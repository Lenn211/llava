┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ COMMON PROBLEMS & QUICK FIXES                                        │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  ❌ "Failed to open camera"
     → Same WiFi? Check both devices!
     → Firewall? Disable temporarily
     → AP Isolation? Check router settings
     → App running? Restart camera app
  
  ❌ "Cannot read frames"
     → Try /videofeed instead of /video
     → Try /mjpegfeed
     → Lower resolution in app settings
     → Move closer to WiFi router
  
  ❌ Laggy/Slow
     → Use 720p or lower resolution
     → Close other apps on phone
     → Use 5GHz WiFi if available
     → Try USB connection instead
